What you actually own

An xStock is a tracker certificate (bearer debt instrument) issued by Backed Assets (JE) Limited, a Jersey company. It tracks the share price and is backed 1:1 by shares held with regulated custodians in segregated accounts.

  • You do not own the share itself.
  • No voting rights, no information rights towards the company.
  • Dividends and stock splits are reflected by automatically adjusting token balances (rebasing).
  • Issuer and platform risk on top of the stock's own risk.
  • Offered in the EEA under a base prospectus approved by Liechtenstein's FMA, through licensed distributors. Not offered in the US, Canada, UK or Australia (per Kraken).
